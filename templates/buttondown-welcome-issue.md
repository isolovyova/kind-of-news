Welcome to Kind of News.

The news can be a lot. This is a small, useful place for things that are
interesting, strange, hopeful, or worth learning - without asking you to keep
your shoulders up around your ears.

The name is a small nod to kindness. Serious things can still be reported
without being cruel to your nervous system.

## ☀️ Good thing

In 1833, Peterborough, New Hampshire opened a free public library supported by
its town and open to everyone. It was a small civic decision with a very large
idea inside it: useful knowledge should not have to belong to a few people.

## 📅 On this day

When Apollo 11 went to the Moon, the crew carried a tiny silicon disc with
messages of goodwill from 73 world leaders. Even at the edge of the known,
someone thought to bring along a little evidence that the rest of the world was
there too.

## 🧠 Tiny fact

Octopuses have three hearts. Two move oxygen-depleted blood through the gills;
the third sends oxygenated blood through the body. It is a strangely elegant
solution to being a very soft animal with a very busy life.

## 🌱 Thought for the day

You do not have to process the whole world to be part of it.
